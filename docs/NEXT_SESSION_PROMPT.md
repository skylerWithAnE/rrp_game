# Prompt for the next session

Written by Claude on 2026-10-05 for the user to paste, and to change first if it is wrong. It
starts a design session, not a build.

---

This is a design session for rrp_game. We are deciding, not building. Do not write or change any
code, and do not compile, until I say a decision is ready to be tried.

Start by reading CLAUDE.md, then docs/DESIGN.md in full, then docs/PLAN.md,
docs/NEXT_PROTOTYPE.md and docs/SESSION_2026-10-05.md. Skim docs/NOTES.md. Don't read
docs/archive/.

Where we are: the mechanics exist as separate test grounds and five small maps, and most of them
I have not played. I set out long-term goals at the end of the last session; they are in
DESIGN.md under "Where it is going", and "What the goals change" lists where the build disagrees
with them. PLAN.md has the agenda I want to work through.

Before we start, tell me in a few lines:
- what you think the game is, from the goals, in your own words
- the three things in the current build that fit the goals least
- anything in the docs that contradicts something else

Then take the agenda in PLAN.md one item at a time, starting with the tiers of road and their
hazards. For each item:
- ask me open questions, one or two at a time, not multiple choice
- before we settle a rule, say what follows from it: what it costs, what it breaks, what it
  makes pointless
- where there are two good answers, say so and describe the station that would let me play
  each, rather than arguing for one
- keep my decisions apart from your proposals, and write my words down as I said them

I answer questions about feel by playing, not on paper. So the output of this session is not a
finished design. It is a short list of stations to build next, each with the one question it
answers, in the order you would build them, and the decisions we did reach written into
DESIGN.md.

Things I already know I want to talk about:
- what a traveller is and what one pays, and whether the first dirt road can pay for the gravel
- what goes wrong on a dirt road, a gravel road and an asphalt road, and how fragile each is
- how one truck or roller is both automated and something I can get in and drive
- painting by hand with a roller brush, and how a line I drew is judged
- how big the map is and how many cities, and whether we need a new kind of ground for it
- which test grounds turn into the real game and which were only for learning

When we finish, update the docs, tell me what you would build first and why, and stop.
