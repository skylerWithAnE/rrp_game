# Session of 2026-10-05

One long session, from about 01:40 to 23:00, in three parts: an unattended push while the user
slept, a day of requests while the user was at work and playing in between, and a rewrite of the
docs at the end. Written by Claude. Twenty commits, none pushed.

## What it started from

The five mechanics stations, played and accepted: stakes and rope, hold-to-click grading with
the hot spot, gravel that packs, and trucks that drive whatever is there. One Stations map.

## What was built

**Overnight, unattended (01:40 to 03:20)**

- **Ground that scales.** The plots' ground is drawn in 8 m square meshes; a stake change works
  out only the sections that changed; trucks find their way by following the ropes.
- **Maps.** Land between two towns, chosen by the host: Short 60 m, Middle 150 m on a slider,
  Long 300 m, Climb. The host sends a height every metre and every machine fills in the 0.25 m
  points with whole-number sums, so the ground cannot differ between machines; only clicked
  points are sent after that. The Long map is 578,000 points and takes 0.3 s to make.
- **Trucks on maps**, setting off from both towns once ropes join them.
- **Two systems of Claude's**, switchable: trucks pack the gravel they drive over, and a click on
  the hot spot also works the rest of its row. By script, 150 m of road went from 13 minutes to 5.
- A rule change with no switch: a truck that gets no further for 4 seconds counts as stuck.

**By day, to the user's requests (10:00 to 22:20)**

- **A slope limit for ropes**, found by sending trucks up ramps: 25 degrees and more on packed
  gravel, 16 on loose, 10 on bare ground. The limit is 15. A red rope now says which rule it breaks.
- **The Switchback map**: rocks nothing can move, lining two banks, so the road has to wind.
- **The Stations map cut into test grounds**: Yard, Building, Trucks, and then Junctions, Quarry,
  Paving and Driving as each was asked for.
- **Junctions**: up to four ropes at a stake. Trucks keep their lane and turn on the junction.
- **Roles and zoning**: service roads that through trucks never use, and a tool to assign them.
- **The quarry**, twice. First a flat one where a truck left by itself when full. Then, to the
  user's second description: a pit dug 8 m into the ground with a winding road, a shovel that is
  loaded with one click and flung with the next, a heap the player places, and trucks that stand
  at depots until they are sent.
- **Paving and painting**, three times over. A dump truck that tipped as it drove; then one with
  a bed a player raised; then, after the user tried it, one that works by itself. Asphalt is
  levelled by shovel or by roller. Lines by a square tool, a free brush, or a driven paint truck.
- **Driving**: a pick-up, a roller, a front loader and a paint truck, simulated on the driver's
  own machine.
- **A shovel in the player's hands**, animated on springs, seen by other players on the blob.
- **A dev tool** that finishes a section's next stage in a click, and **flying**.

**At the end**

- `DESIGN.md` rewritten as the game stands; the morning report and the old design page left to
  git history, at the user's word that no archive folder is wanted.
- The user's long-term goals recorded, `PLAN.md` rewritten around them, and a prompt written for
  the next design session.

## What went wrong

- **The dump truck could not be clicked** with the stake, zoning or dev tool in hand, and stakes
  is what a player starts with. Claude had checked it by calling the host's function, which
  proved the rule and never touched the control. Fixed; the lesson is in `NOTES.md`.
- **Its bed swung nose down.** Not looked at in a screenshot before it was called done.
- **Getting out of a vehicle launched it** 225 m: the blob was put down inside it. Found in
  Claude's own check, and fixed.
- **The dump truck shuttled for ever**, because it never follows its lane exactly and always
  left slivers. It now stops when a tenth or less is bare.
- **The first ramps on the Trucks ground showed nothing**: 20 m is short enough to coast over.
- **A patch script cut out two functions it was not aimed at**, twice. Both restored from git
  before anything was built on them.
- **Too much was built between plays.** By evening about ten systems stood on three that had
  been played.

## What is known and not fixed

- A client whose process is killed, not closed, makes the host stop hearing everyone about 30
  seconds later. Not caused by this session's code; cause not found.
- A truck that is sent is put at the start of its way, because there is nowhere to turn round.
- Since the slope limit, a road staked straight over the Middle map's hill may be refused. Seen
  once by script, not confirmed by hand.
- The roller on the Paving ground wrecked once while parked. Not seen again after a later change.
